Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 17, 2026

EasyDownloader ("EasyDownloader", "we", "our", or "us") provides tools that help users open supported websites, parse video or image links, and download media that the user has the right to access. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use our Android application.

1. Summary

We do not require you to create an EasyDownloader account, and we do not sell your personal information. Most app data, such as download records, browsing shortcuts, bookmarks, search history, and platform login status, is stored on your device. When you ask the app to parse or download a link, the app may connect to the relevant third-party website or service so that the requested media information can be found.

2. Information You Provide or Generate

  • Links and shared text. When you paste, type, scan, share, or open a URL with EasyDownloader, the app processes that URL to decide whether it can be opened, parsed, or downloaded.
  • Downloads and local records. The app may store download tasks, file names, file paths, progress, completed download records, bookmarks, browser tabs, visited pages, and search history locally on your device so the app can show your download list and restore your browsing state.
  • Platform login status. Some supported platforms may require your own logged-in session to parse content you can access. If you choose to log in or import a platform cookie, EasyDownloader stores the required cookies only on your device using Android encrypted storage. We do not upload, sell, or share these cookies with our own server. They are used only to request media information from the relevant platform on your behalf.
  • Feedback. If you contact us by email, we may receive your email address, name, message, screenshots, logs, or other information you choose to include. We use this information only to respond to you, troubleshoot issues, and improve the app.

3. Link Parsing, Short Links, and Downloads

To provide video and image parsing, EasyDownloader may send network requests to the website contained in the link you submit, such as TikTok, X/Twitter, Facebook, Instagram, Vimeo, Dailymotion, Pinterest, Streamable, IMDb, or other supported websites. These requests may include the submitted URL, normal browser-like request headers, cookies required by that website, and technical information needed to resolve short links or retrieve downloadable media metadata.

Download requests are made to the media host identified from the link. Some platforms require temporary headers or cookies for the download request to work. EasyDownloader uses them for the requested download and does not use them for advertising or unrelated purposes.

EasyDownloader does not support downloading videos from YouTube. You are responsible for ensuring that your use of downloaded content complies with applicable laws and the terms of the relevant website.

4. WebView, Cookies, and Third-Party Websites

EasyDownloader includes a browser/WebView experience for supported workflows. Websites opened inside the app may set their own cookies, local storage, or similar technologies according to their own privacy policies. EasyDownloader is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites.

Platform cookies saved through EasyDownloader's platform login feature are stored in encrypted local app storage and are excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer where supported. You can clear the relevant login status in the app, clear app data, or uninstall the app to remove locally stored data.

5. Clipboard and QR Code Scanning

If you choose to paste from the clipboard, use a clipboard shortcut, or use a link-detection workflow while the app is active, EasyDownloader may read clipboard text to find a supported URL. Clipboard content is not uploaded unless you submit that link for parsing, opening, or downloading.

If you use QR code scanning, the app requests camera access to scan the code. Camera data is used for scanning and is not stored by us.

6. Permissions We Request

  • Internet and network state: used to open websites, resolve short links, parse media, download files, load ads, fetch remote configuration, and check network availability.
  • Storage/media access: used on supported Android versions to save downloaded files and play or manage downloaded media.
  • Camera: used only when you choose QR code scanning.
  • Foreground service: used to keep download work running reliably when needed.
  • Vibration: used for lightweight user feedback such as scanning or interaction feedback.

7. Analytics, Crash Reports, Ads, and Remote Configuration

EasyDownloader uses third-party services to keep the app reliable, measure basic app usage, show ads, deliver configuration, and diagnose crashes. These providers may collect device identifiers, advertising identifiers, app interaction events, crash logs, diagnostics, approximate location derived from IP address, device information, and other data described in their own policies.

Third-party services used by the app may include:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Firebase, including Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Remote Config, and Firebase Cloud Messaging

You can reset or limit use of your advertising ID through your Android or Google settings where available. Ad personalization choices may also be available through Google settings.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you delete it, clear app data, remove individual records where the app provides that option, or uninstall EasyDownloader. Platform login cookies are stored locally and can be cleared by clearing the relevant login status, clearing app data, or uninstalling the app. Information you send to us by email is retained only as long as reasonably needed to respond to your request and maintain support records.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect local sensitive data, including Android encrypted storage for platform login cookies. However, no app, network transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children

EasyDownloader is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

11. International Transfers

Third-party services used by the app, such as Google and Firebase services, may process information on servers located in countries outside your country of residence. Their processing is governed by their own privacy policies and data protection terms.

12. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to certain processing of your personal information. Most EasyDownloader data is stored locally on your device and can be deleted by using in-app controls, clearing app data, or uninstalling the app. For support requests or privacy questions, contact us at the email address below.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of EasyDownloader after an update means you accept the updated policy.

14. Contact Us
