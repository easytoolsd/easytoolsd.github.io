1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Simple Quote & Invoice Maker ("the App") handles your personal information. We take your privacy seriously and are committed to keeping your data safe.

Short version: We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data. All information you enter into the App stays on your device. There is no server-side data collection, no user accounts, and no tracking.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Data You Enter

The App allows you to create and store quotes, invoices, and client records. All of this data is stored locally on your device using an on-device database (Room / SQLite). None of this data is transmitted to our servers or any third-party servers.

This includes:

  • Client names, phone numbers, email addresses, and physical addresses
  • Quote and invoice details (line items, amounts, dates, statuses)
  • Business profile information (business name, logo, signature, contact details)
  • Generated PDF documents

2.2 Device Information

The App does not collect device identifiers, hardware information, or any diagnostic data.

2.3 Usage Analytics

The App does not track your usage patterns, screen views, or feature engagement. We do not use analytics SDKs to monitor your behavior within the App.

2.4 Crash Reports

[Optional] If you choose to enable crash reporting, anonymized crash logs may be collected via Firebase Crashlytics to help us diagnose and fix app stability issues. These logs do not contain your personal data, quotes, invoices, or client information. You can disable this at any time through your device settings.

3. How We Use Your Information

Since we do not collect or transmit your data, we do not use your personal information for any purpose. All data management is handled entirely on your device:

  • Creating and storing quotes and invoices
  • Generating PDF documents for you to share
  • Searching and filtering your documents
  • Tracking payment statuses

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not share, sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your data to any third parties.

The only way your data leaves your device is when you voluntarily share a generated PDF document through your device's native sharing features (e.g., email, messaging apps). This action is entirely under your control.

5. Data Security

Your data is protected by the security of your device. We recommend:

  • Using a device lock (PIN, pattern, fingerprint, or face recognition)
  • Keeping your device's operating system up to date
  • Backing up your device regularly, as the App data is stored locally and could be lost if the device is damaged or erased

The App does not implement additional encryption beyond the standard Android data protection mechanisms.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data remains on your device until you choose to delete it:

  • Individual records: You can delete specific quotes, invoices, or client records at any time through the App's interface.
  • All data: Uninstalling the App will remove all associated data from your device.
  • There is no server-side data to request deletion from, as we do not store any of your information remotely.

7. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party services that collect personal data. The following applies:

  • PDF Sharing: When you share a PDF, it uses Android's native sharing sheet. The receiving app's privacy policy governs that data transfer.
  • Google Play: Google may collect anonymous download and usage statistics as described in the Google Privacy Policy.
  • [Optional] Firebase Crashlytics: If enabled, collects anonymized crash diagnostics only. See Firebase Privacy.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is designed for adult service professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Since the App does not collect any data whatsoever, this policy inherently protects all users regardless of age.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
