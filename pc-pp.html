Photo Collage Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 27, 2026 · Last updated: September 12, 2026

Photo Collage (“the app”) is provided by EasyTools. This policy applies to the Android app Photo Collage (package: com.easytools.photocollage) and explains how it handles photos, files and other information.

1. Local photo processing

Photo Collage processes selected photos locally on your device. Photos are not uploaded to EasyTools servers. The app does not require an account.

2. Photo access

The app uses the Android system Photo Picker, with the system document picker as a fallback. It also accepts photos you explicitly share from other apps. You choose the specific photos that the app can access. The app does not request broad access to your photo library.

3. Drafts, cache and exports

  • When you create a draft, selected source photos may be copied to the app’s private storage so the draft can be restored.
  • Incoming shared photos are copied to private storage before editing. Export task parameters and progress are stored locally so interrupted tasks can be recovered.
  • Temporary decoded images and processing files may be stored in the app cache.
  • On Android 10 and later, images or PDFs are saved to shared device storage when you explicitly export them. On older Android versions, exports are held privately until you choose a destination using Save to device.
  • Draft source copies and image caches are excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer.

You can delete drafts in Projects, clear temporary files in Settings, delete exported images in the viewer, or remove all private app data by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android settings.

4. Data collection and sharing

The current version does not automatically send your photos, editing text, file names, file locations, device identifiers, usage analytics, crash reports or advertising data to EasyTools or third parties. EasyTools does not sell your personal information. User-requested sharing and email correspondence are described separately below. The app does not include advertising, analytics or cloud photo-processing SDKs.

5. User-initiated sharing

When you tap Share, Android’s system share sheet sends the selected export to the app or person you choose. That transfer is initiated and controlled by you and is subject to the recipient’s privacy practices.

6. Permissions

The app does not request READ_MEDIA_IMAGES or READ_MEDIA_VIDEO. It relies on Android’s Photo Picker, MediaStore, and the system document picker for selected inputs and user-requested exports. Long processing tasks use a foreground service with a progress notification. Notification permission is requested only when starting a multiple-image task; declining it does not block processing.

7. Retention, deletion and your choices

Local drafts and their source copies remain until you delete them or clear the app’s storage. The app does not automatically delete drafts merely because you have created more than twenty projects. Imported shared images, privately staged exports and export recovery records may remain in private storage until that storage is cleared. Temporary caches may be removed by the app or Android.

Deleting a draft removes its app-managed copy, not the original photo you selected. Clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling removes private app data, but does not remove exports already saved to your gallery, a document provider or another app. Delete those files using the gallery, file manager or destination service. Copies already shared with others are controlled by their recipients.

You can stop selecting photos, cancel processing tasks, manage notification permission in Android settings, delete individual drafts, or clear all private app data. We cannot retrieve or delete files that exist only on your device on your behalf.

8. Android backup and external services

The app’s backup configuration permits preferences, such as theme, language and export defaults, to be backed up or transferred through Android. Private photo copies, drafts and generated files are excluded from the configured app backup and device transfer; task recovery records are stored in backup-excluded storage. Android backup depends on your device settings and system provider.

A photo or document provider you choose may store files in the cloud under its own terms. Gallery backup, email services, the Android share destination and Google Play operate under their own privacy policies. The app does not control their independent processing.

9. Support correspondence

If you choose to email EasyTools, your email service sends your email address, message and any attachments you select. We use that correspondence to respond to your request and resolve support or privacy issues. Please avoid sending photos or other sensitive information unless needed for your request.

Support correspondence is kept only as needed to handle the request, related follow-up or applicable legal obligations. You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information you have supplied to us. Rights available to you depend on applicable law; we will handle your request accordingly. The app has no user accounts to delete.

10. Children

The app is a general-audience utility and is not directed specifically to children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Security

Processing is performed locally and private drafts are stored in the app sandbox. No method of storage is completely risk-free, so you should protect access to your device and remove sensitive drafts when they are no longer needed.

12. Changes

If the app later adds advertising, analytics, cloud processing or other data practices, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration will be updated before those features are released.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact EasyTools at EasyToolsd@outlook.com.
