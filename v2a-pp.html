Privacy Policy for Video to MP3 & Audio Editor

Last updated: July 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android application Video to MP3 & Audio Editor, also displayed within the application as Smart Audio Extractor, with package name com.dg.videotoaudio.extractor.

The application is developed and published on Google Play by EasyMedia Studio ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we access, collect, use, share, retain, and protect information when you use the application.

1. Local Processing

All audio extraction, conversion, and trimming operations are performed entirely on your device. Your media files are never uploaded to any server. We do not have access to your files.

2. Information We Collect

We do not ask you to provide your name, email address, or phone number. The App uses third-party services that may automatically collect the following data:

  • Usage analytics: Anonymous app usage data (screens visited, features used) via Firebase Analytics to improve the App.
  • Crash reports: Anonymous crash and error reports via Firebase Crashlytics to fix bugs.
  • Advertising data: Google AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve relevant ads.

3. Permissions

The App requests the following permissions:

  • Storage / Media access: To read video and audio files you select, and to save converted audio files to your device.
  • Notifications: To show audio playback controls and conversion progress in the notification bar.

We only use these permissions for the stated purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services that may collect data according to their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics
  • Google AdMob

5. Data Security

Since all processing happens locally on your device, your media files remain under your control. We do not transmit, store, or have access to your files.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Media files processed by the App remain on your device and are not uploaded to servers operated by us. You can delete generated audio files at any time using the App or your device's file manager.

Analytics, crash-reporting, and advertising data may be retained by Google according to Google's applicable retention policies. We do not maintain user accounts or directly store personal data on our own servers. For privacy or deletion requests, contact us at EasyToolsd@outlook.com.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will be published at this URL and displayed through the App.
