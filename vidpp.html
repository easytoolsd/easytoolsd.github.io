Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 12, 2026

Summary: VidPro processes your video files locally on your device. We do not upload your videos, file names, file paths, or raw file URIs. We use Firebase services for app analytics, crash diagnostics, and remote configuration so we can improve stability and tune app behavior.

1. Introduction

Welcome to VidPro - Video Toolbox ("VidPro", "we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile application.

2. Local Video Processing

All video processing, including compression, conversion, repair, rotation, and trimming, is performed on your device. Your video files and video content are not uploaded to our servers.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

VidPro does not collect or upload:

  • Your video files or video content
  • File names, absolute file paths, or raw content URIs
  • Your name, email address, phone number, contacts, messages, or precise location
  • Advertising IDs for ad targeting

4. App Analytics and Diagnostics

We use Firebase Analytics to understand broad app behavior, such as which features are opened, whether processing succeeds or fails, and how long key flows take. These events use aggregated technical metadata such as feature type, file extension, file size range, duration, codec choices, and processing result. They do not include video content, file names, absolute paths, or raw URIs.

We use Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports and non-fatal error diagnostics. Crash reports may include device and app state needed to debug failures, such as app version, Android version, feature type, error code, and processing stage.

5. Remote Configuration

We use Firebase Remote Config to adjust app behavior without requiring an app update, such as compression defaults, feature toggles, and safety kill switches. Remote Config does not upload your video files.

6. Permissions We Request

  • Storage/Media Permission: Used to read videos you select and save processed output files.
  • Foreground Service: Used to keep video processing running reliably while the app is in the background.
  • Wake Lock: Used to reduce the chance of processing interruption while a task is active.

7. Output Files

Processed video files are saved on your device in the export directory you choose, such as Movies/VidPro. You can preview, share, rename, move, or delete these files.

8. Third-Party Services

  • FFmpeg SDK (videohandler): Local video processing library.
  • Firebase Analytics: App usage analytics.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Crash and diagnostic reporting.
  • Firebase Remote Config: Remote configuration and feature controls.
  • Android framework libraries: Standard Android platform components.

9. Children's Privacy

VidPro is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last Updated" date when changes are made.
